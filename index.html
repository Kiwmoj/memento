    About microphone access

    The first time you use voice, your browser asks for microphone permission. Choose Allow. If you blocked it, click the lock icon beside the address bar, set Microphone to Allow, then reload.

    Voice uses your browser’s built-in speech recognition where available (Chrome and Edge may process audio with their own speech service). Other browsers, such as Firefox, record from your microphone and transcribe on this computer using an offline model. Memento never stores audio. Typed questions stay entirely on this device.
